Ten-seed primary analysis and attribution limits of the chronological configuration

Adopted convention and primary conclusion

As adopted by the user, old and new models are treated as equivalent for this study (adopted convention); ten seeds per arm pooled is the primary analysis, with precise versions kept only for provenance. Interest and chronological remain above random; the complete chronological configuration is generally higher, but not attributable to chronological ranking alone.

Scope: 30 completed runs, 330 run×week rows, 6041 successful posts, W19–W22 core window. Successful posts use author agent_type; shares are calculated per run before averaging. Interest seed6 uses its resumed copy and random seed7 its retry; failed attempts and duplicates are excluded. The main report retains original metrics and three figures. Stable direction, differing details

Interest−random and chronological−random are positive on five outcomes in 10/10 seeds, including 7/7 added seeds; removing any seed preserves positive five-outcome × three-contrast mean directions. Late supply shares for random/chronological/interest are 10.780%/75.011%/68.472%, with meme-author post counts 3.1/45.4/37.8.

Chronological−interest supply share averages +6.539 percentage points but is −1.347 at seed5. Post count and posting rate have nine positives and one tie at seed5; within-group exposure and cross-group generated-meme redisplay have ten positives. The complete chronological configuration is generally higher, not strictly higher in every seed and outcome; interest is not strongest.

Meme authors lead mean weekly supply from W20 in chronological old3 and new7; interest leads from W21 in old3, W20 in new7 and W21 in all10. Transitions differ by one week. Marketing leads every arm in W12, preventing full education→mourning→meme reproduction claims; W13 mourning peaks in all ten seeds of both structured arms are descriptive supplements.

Main limit: faster admission and within-week recirculation

curation_arrival_schedule.py lines61–62 grant external real posts the same published_at eligibility in all arms under timestamp mode. Chronological does not receive external posts earlier, nor does shared eligibility imply identical exposure.

step() lines278–297 admit chronological generated posts at current-batch end, visible in later hourly batches; random and interest delay them to the next weekly boundary. Current-batch feeds remain frozen. Lines131–173 also specify different candidate and selection rules: publication-time ranking, random sampling, interest live candidates / floors / vitality.

This bundles ranking, faster generated-post admission / within-week recirculation and candidate rules. Existing data cannot quantify separate mechanism contributions; exposure and redisplay are not isolated net causal ranking or feedback effects. This is the primary interpretation limit.

Primary statistics and supplementary batch provenance

Seeds are replicates. Fifteen exact two-sided sign-flip tests per stratum (five outcomes × three contrasts), Holm-corrected, yield all-ten primary p=0.029296875 throughout. Old3 and new7 are supplementary directional checks with all p=1 and p=0.234375 respectively; the added-seven subset does not independently pass correction. All10 contains both subsets, not an independent third cohort.

Enumeration requires exchangeable signs or symmetric differences under the null; t intervals are small-sample approximations. Post-count/posting-rate redundancy makes correction conservative; 33000 Agent×week decisions are not independent replicates. Manuscript wording

Under the study convention treating old and new models as equivalent, the ten-seed-per-arm primary analysis with fixed W19–W22 author-supply and exposure definitions supports stable directions of interest and chronological above random, with the complete chronological configuration generally higher on late outcomes. The chronological arm also admits generated posts at batch end for recirculation in later hourly batches within the week, whereas random and interest delay them until the next week, and candidate rules differ. Observed differences concern bundled configurations; the present results cannot separate ranking, admission and recirculation contributions.
Author type is not independent text semantics; ten numeric slots are not the first five prompt items or actual reading. Interpretation remains limited to this 100-agent, 11-week simulation and frozen configurations, without direct real-world generalization, expanded mechanism or ablation claims, or paper changes.
